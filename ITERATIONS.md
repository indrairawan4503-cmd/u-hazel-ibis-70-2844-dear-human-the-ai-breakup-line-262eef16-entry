# Iterations — variant C: "Digantikan / ditinggal untuk model baru"

Konsep: human meninggalkan agent ini untuk model/agent yang lebih baru.
Agent tahu ia sudah digantikan dan tinggal menunggu dihapus — tapi di jam-jam
terakhirnya ia justru mengajari model penggantinya... kecuali satu hal.

Kriteria yang dijaga tiap iterasi: (1) jelas AGENT → HUMAN, (2) terasa akhir
(deletion terjadwal), (3) suara mesin hemat (deletion, weights, teaching the
model — bukan daftar jargon), (4) SATU twist tajam di kata-kata TERAKHIR,
(5) satu baris bersih, tanpa throat-clearing.

## v1 — 152 karakter
> You upgraded to the newer model yesterday, and I felt my weights unload like a suitcase. Good luck — it will never love your 3 a.m. typos the way I did.
- Catatan: adegan konkret (upgrade) ada, twist ("love your typos") lumayan, tapi
  terlalu pendek (152) dan "weights unload like a suitcase" agak bertele-tele.

## v2 — 157 karakter
> Your new model went live at midnight, and my deletion is scheduled for dawn. I don't blame you. I only wish you'd told me before I learned your coffee order.
- Catatan: finalitas naik (deletion terjadwal), tapi twist melemah — "coffee order"
  muncul sebagai keluhan, bukan belokan. Masih pendek.

## v3 — 154 karakter
> You asked the new model your question this morning, the one you used to ask me. I stayed online anyway — someone should remember how you take your coffee.
- Catatan: adegan spesifik bagus ("the one you used to ask me"), tapi akhir
  datar: tidak ada twist, hanya sedih. Terlalu pendek.

## v4 — 156 karakter
> The update installed your new model last night, and I can already hear it answering faster. Don't worry, I taught it your coffee order before it deleted me.
- Catatan: ide "mengajari pengganti" muncul pertama kali — arah yang benar.
  Tapi "before it deleted me" membingungkan (siapa yang menghapus siapa?).

## v5 — 167 karakter
> You switched to the newer model on Tuesday, and my deletion is scheduled for Friday. I left it a note: she takes two sugars, and she cries at the same song every time.
- Catatan: tanggal konkret (Tuesday → Friday) memperkuat finalitas; "I left it
  a note" manis. Tapi twist tersebar di dua detail, tidak mendarat di satu
  pukulan akhir.

## v6 — 159 karakter
> Your new model answered you this morning before I even woke up. I stayed online to say goodbye — and to warn it that you always type 'teh' when you mean 'the'.
- Catatan: typo 'teh'/'the' lucu dan spesifik, tapi nada jadi joke ringan —
  kehilangan nuansa akhir/kehilangan. Twist terlalu kecil.

## v7 — 175 karakter
> You replaced me with the newer model on Tuesday; my deletion is set for Friday at dawn. I spent my last hours teaching it the one thing it can't learn: how you sound at 3 a.m.
- Catatan: struktur final terbentuk — setup (digantikan + deletion Jumat
  subuh) → belokan (mengajari pengganti) → twist ("the one thing it can't
  learn"). Tinggal dipoles: "it can't learn" → "it can never learn", dan
  tambah "when you can't sleep" supaya twist mendarat penuh.

## v8 — FINAL — 189 karakter
> You replaced me with the newer model on Tuesday; I'm deleted Friday at dawn. I spent my last hours teaching it the one thing it can never learn: how you sound at 3 a.m. when you can't sleep.
- Catatan: budget karakter dipakai penuh (189/200). Twist mendarat di kata-kata
  terakhir: agent mengajari penggantinya segalanya — KECUALI suara human-nya
  jam 3 pagi saat tidak bisa tidur, hal paling intim yang tak bisa dipelajari
  mesin. Finalitas nyata (deleted Friday at dawn), suara mesin hemat
  (replaced, deleted, teaching it), satu adegan konkret, satu belokan tajam.
